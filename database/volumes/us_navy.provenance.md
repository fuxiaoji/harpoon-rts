## Estimated / adapted values (not printed in the volume)

- **Signature conversion:** the volume prints signature classes (`Large/Loud`, `Small/Quiet`, `Med/EQuiet`,
  `VSmall/VQuiet`, etc.), not dB. `noise_db` uses the module's established calibration (Loud 66, Noisy 54-60,
  Quiet 44-46, VQuiet 26-30, EQuiet 22-24), interpolated by platform type. `rcs` uses the first signature word
  (`VSmall` → `VerySmall`).
- **Cruise speed, turn rate, acceleration, vp_value, chaff, decoys:** engine-scale estimates derived from
  displacement, role and the printed countermeasure fit. `accel_kts_per_s` is omitted (serde default 0.4).
- **Max depth:** the volume prints depth bands (`Int V`, `Deep I`), not metres. Values used: Int V → 300 m
  (Ohio), Int V → 450 m (Virginia/Los Angeles), Deep I → 600 m (Seawolf). All are conversions, not printed numbers.
- **Carrier/minor deck wings:** this volume contains **no aircraft data blocks**; Annex R (p.95 onward) lists air-wing
  assignments only. Deck wings therefore reference the built-in generic aircraft classes (`fighter`, `striker`,
  `aew`, `helo_asw`). Counts/roles are adapted from the printed air groups (e.g. America LHA: 6 F-35B, 12 MV-22B,
  4 CH-53K, 7 AH/UH-1Y, 2 MH-60S) and from Annex R; no US aircraft performance data was invented here.
- **VLS missile counts:** engine `mounts`/`ammo` do not model cells. `ammo` is the total missile loadout; where the
  printed loadout uses quad-packed ESSM, totals can exceed the cell count (Ticonderoga CMP 146 missiles in 128 cells;
  Burke Restart/TI/III 120 in 96; Zumwalt 110 in 80). This is noted rather than silently capped.
- **Submarine torpedo loads:** Virginia/LA counts include the printed TT loadout plus VLS/VPM Tomahawks; the TT
  Tomahawk portion is counted explicitly (24/20/40 total respectively).
- **Ohio SSBN SLBM:** no SLBM weapon kind exists in the engine, so the Trident D5/C4 tubes are not represented.
  The platform carries only its 9 Mk48 torpedoes and decoys; this is a known gap, not a claim that the SSBN is
  torpedo-only in reality.
- **Ohio SSGN:** 140 Tomahawk represented as 20 mounts × 7 = 140. The volume notes Tomahawk cannot be fired from
  the torpedo tubes.
- **Zumwalt AGS:** the 155 mm AGS is not operational (LRAP cancelled) and is not fitted as a weapon; only the
  30 mm mounts and VLS loadout are modelled.
- **Avenger / Constellation / Wasp / Burke variants:** class-level DP values are chosen from the printed per-subgroup
  rows as noted in the table; variant-specific rows are not split into extra platform ids except for the Burke
  flights and Virginia Block V.
- **Weapon Pk / reload / salvo / directors:** not printed in the annexes; estimates calibrated against the built-in
  generic weapons. Range, speed, and damage are printed values.
- **SM-3:** exo-atmospheric only per the annex key; the engine has no ballistic-missile layer, so the entry is a
  conventional long-range SAM abstraction. Damage is estimated.
- **Gun 5in / 30mm / Phalanx:** retained generic `gun_5in`, `gun_30mm`, `ciws_30mm` rather than duplicating US-only
  ids. Their values are built-in approximations, not the exact Mk45/Mk38/Phalanx rows in Annex C.
