# commonwealth_aircraft.json — provenance

**Source volume:** `16-1.（数据库）Commonwealth_Aircraft_1_Dec_25.pdf`
(Admiralty Trilogy Group, *Commonwealth Aircraft*, 1 Jan 24 printing with
1 Dec 25 digital update; 99 PDF pages).

**Transcription source:** PDF page text extracted with macOS PDFKit
(`swift /tmp/pdftext.swift <pdf> <first> <last>`), which prints the PDF page
number before each page's text. The PDF page number is one less than the
printed Annex-B page number (PDF p. 6 = printed B-1); each citation below
gives both.

**Scope actually present in the volume:** United Kingdom, Australia, Canada
and New Zealand. The volume contains no Indian aircraft section; searches of
the full extracted text for “Rafale” and “MiG-29K” return no Annex-B entry
(see Notes).

**Mapping used for the platform fields**

- `hp` = the entry's printed **Damage Value** (2012 Standard, per the volume's
  Annex Notes on printed B-2).
- `cruise_speed_kts` = the first **Cruise** figure in the throttle table (the
  Low-altitude row); `max_speed_kts` = the highest **Full Mil/Reheat** figure
  in the table (combat maximum, not cruise).
- `rcs` = the second value of **Size/Signature**, mapped
  `Large→Large`, `Medium→Medium`, `Small→Small`, `VSmall→VerySmall`; the
  exact per-entry source value is in the table above.
- `endurance_s` = **Cruise Range (nmi) ÷ cruise speed (kt) × 3600**, rounded
  to the nearest second. This is a derivation from two printed values, not a
  printed endurance figure.
- `name` carries the printed **Ceiling** because the schema has no notes field.
- `sensors`/`weapons` are fit selections from each entry's Sensors and
  Ordnance Loadouts lines; non-weapon sensor names (radar, FLIR, ESM, etc.)
  are mapped to the generic/volume sensor ids listed below.

## Estimated values (not printed as such in the volume)

The following were inferred/calibrated and are **not** direct data-book
numbers. Everything else above is a printed value or a direct arithmetic
derivation from printed values.

1. **Turn rate (`turn_rate_deg_s`), acceleration (`accel_kts_per_s`), VP
   value (`vp_value`), chaff and decoy counts** for every platform. The volume
   prints manoeuvre ratings and countermeasure generations, not these engine
   quantities. Turn rates were banded by role (fighters 8–14, helicopters
   10–20, MPA/AEW 4, transports 3, UAV 6); VP ≈ 0.45/hp for combat aircraft,
   0.40/hp for MPA/AEW, 0.35/hp for ASW helicopters, 0.25/hp for transport
   helicopters, 0.15/hp for transports; chaff/decoys 8–16 for combat types,
   20–30 for MPA/AEW, 0–8 for transports.
2. **AAM range and Pk.** The work order specifies `cw_asraam`/`cw_meteor`
   as Aam, Pk 0.65, range 40 nm, estimated. The volume's Annex H4 lists max
   ranges of 4.0 nmi (ASRAAM) and 3.5/4.0 nmi (Meteor); the 40 nm figure is
   the engine-scale abstraction mandated by the work order and is recorded
   here as estimated. `cw_air_amraam` range 35 nm / Pk 0.60 is the same
   engine-scale abstraction. AAM damage 20 follows the built-in `aam_ir`
   convention (the volume gives warhead/ATA, not damage points).
3. **ASM/torpedo Pk.** `cw_harpoon` Pk 0.55, `cw_sea_venom` Pk 0.60,
   `cw_air_sea_eagle`/`cw_air_sea_skua` Pk 0.55, `cw_stingray`/`cw_mk46` Pk 0.55,
   `cw_mk54` Pk 0.60. Annexes H2/F give range, speed and damage but no
   terminal probability.
4. **Torpedo `enable_range_nm`** 0.5 for all lightweight torpedoes
   (engine seeker-activation convention; not in Annex F).
5. **`cw_gun_20mm`** range 2.0 nmi, Pk 0.15, salvo 3, reload 4 s, damage 6.
   Annex H3 gives calibre and ATA only; damage is scaled against the built-in
   30 mm gun (damage 8).
6. **New sensor ranges** `cw_radar_mpa`, `cw_radar_helo`, `cw_flir`,
   `cw_air_sonobuoy`, `cw_air_mad` — see the sensor table. They are abstractions of
   the Annex J3/K2 class data, not single printed line entries.
7. **Endurance** — derived from cruise range and cruise speed as described
   above (the volume's `Cruise Range` is not always at the same throttle
   setting); treat as a derived, not printed, value.
8. **Loadout quantities** — where the volume gives alternative loadouts, the
   JSON stores one representative maximum (e.g. 4 Harpoon for F/A-18F,
   6 Sting Ray + 3 Harpoon for Nimrod, 4 Sting Ray for Merlin). Some of
   these mix alternative listed fits (Nimrod's 6 Sting Ray and 3 Harpoon are
   separate loadouts); they are engine loadout selections, not new numbers.
   Earlier/later Harpoon variants are split into `cw_harpoon_a` (AGM-84A,
   60 nmi), `cw_harpoon` (AGM-84D, 80 nmi) and `cw_harpoon_er` (AGM-84G,
   130 nmi) so the fit matches the entry.
9. **Magazine/burst counts** (`gun_30mm` 100–150, `cw_gun_20mm` 400–500)
   are engine ammunition abstractions; the volume gives gun ratings, not
   round counts.

## Notes and omissions

- **Rafale-M and MiG-29K are not in this volume.** The 1 Dec 25
  *Commonwealth Aircraft* file covers the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada
  and New Zealand (title page and Annex B sections on PDF pp. 6, 19, 28, 32).
  A full-text search of all 99 extracted pages found no Rafale-M or MiG-29K
  Annex-B entry; the only “Rafale” hit is a tanker clearance note on PDF
  p. 67 (printed B-62), and “India as FRS.51” on PDF p. 56 is a Sea Harrier
  export note. They were therefore **not** transcribed; inventing entries
  would violate the transcription rules.
- **New Zealand prefix.** The work order's prefix set is
  `cw_/uk_/au_/ca_/in_` and has no `nz_`; New Zealand platforms therefore use
  the volume prefix `cw_` (`cw_p8a_nz`, `cw_nh90_tth`, `cw_sh2g_nz`,
  `cw_c130j_nz`). No platform id collides with a weapon or sensor id.
- **`cw_air_` ids.** The five aircraft-weapon/sensor ids `cw_air_amraam`,
  `cw_air_sea_eagle`, `cw_air_sea_skua`, `cw_air_mad` and `cw_air_sonobuoy`
  carry an `air` category segment so they cannot collide with the
  `cw_amraam`/`cw_sea_eagle`/`cw_sea_skua`/`cw_mad`/`cw_sonobuoy` entries in
  the separate Commonwealth navies volume; all still carry the `cw_` volume
  prefix. A programmatic overlap check against every other
  `database/volumes/*.json` file present at transcription time returned no
  shared platform, weapon or sensor ids.
- **Reused ids.** `aam_ir`, `gun_30mm`, `radar_air_xlong`, `radar_air_long`,
  `radar_air_med`, `radar_surf_med`, `radar_surf_sm`, `radar_nav`,
  `esm_std`, `visual`, `sonar_dipping`, `bomb_mk82` and `rocket_57` are
  existing database ids and are not redefined here. (`bomb_mk82` and
  `rocket_57` come from the engine's unguided-ordnance layer, not this
  volume.)
- **Hellfire, Martlet, Brimstone, Paveway, JDAM, Storm Shadow, Spear 3 and
  Penguin** appear in loadout lines but were not given new weapon ids: the
  schema has no accurate kind/guidance for anti-armour or guided-bomb
  weapons, and the task forbids semantically incorrect new weapons. The
  representative loadouts therefore use the generic/volume anti-ship,
  torpedo, AAM and unguided-bomb entries that fit the schema.
- **Data-book-estimated fields.** The volume itself marks several figures
  “estimated” (e.g. Merlin Crowsnest speeds and range, CH-148 sonobuoy
  count, various sonobuoy loadouts). Those printed estimates are used as
  printed; they are flagged in the citations above where relevant.
