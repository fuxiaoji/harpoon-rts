# us_f18e_asm: Super Hornet anti-ship mission fit

Source: `4-4.Americas_Aircraft__1_Dec_25.pdf`, 1 Dec 2025 digital update, PDF p.49 / printed B-44, F/A-18E/F Super Hornet Standard Loadouts. Original page visually reviewed on 2026-10-19; the listed three-drop-tank alternative carries 2 AIM-9, 2 AIM-120 and 2 AGM-84D Harpoon IC (with other dated alternatives).

`us_f18e_asm` copies all airframe fields from the existing `us_f18e` volume entry and replaces only the mission weapon fit: 2 × `us_aim9x`, 2 × `us_aim120`, 2 × `us_agm84`. The AIM-9X choice and allocation to these fictional carrier wings are scenario assumptions. Missile definitions are unchanged; AGM-84D comes from the same book PDF p.99 / H2-1 and the existing `us_aircraft.provenance.md` record.

This is an additional loadout variant, not a new independently verified airframe. It inherits every estimated/adapted field documented in `us_aircraft.provenance.md`; do not count it as a new source-verified platform or advertise the exercise as strict Harpoon fidelity. The original bomb-equipped `us_f18e` remains unchanged. Generated by `scripts/make-dual-carrier-scenario.py`; database total becomes 408 platform entries (407 prior entries plus this mission fit), 264 weapons and 194 sensors.
